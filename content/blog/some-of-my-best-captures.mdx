Most of what I point a camera at ends up being sky. A ridge holding the last light, monsoon clouds stacking over a valley, or a road that disappears exactly where the horizon begins.

This is a running collection of those frames — the ones that survived the camera roll. The set below is a sketchbook stand-in while the full-resolution archive gets sorted; the captions are the notes I kept.
