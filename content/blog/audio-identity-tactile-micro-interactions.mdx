The human ear processes auditory signals in under 10 milliseconds — more than twice as fast as visual stimuli.

Yet for two decades, web interfaces have been completely deaf and mute. We click sterile plastic screens without tactile or acoustic feedback.

Integrating the Web Audio API does not mean playing obnoxious MP3 sound effects. It means synthesizing micro-acoustic harmonics that confirm user actions with pleasant, warm resonance.

When a user taps "Horn OK Please" on this site, we synthesize dual harmonic frequencies with a gentle band-pass filter and compressor, evoking the iconic brass horn with zero external audio assets.
